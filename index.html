RadioWorld

Transferring World Models to Radio-Frequency Radiance Fields

Hu Zhou1, Jiazhou Zhou2, Shuowen Zhang1, Ying-Chang Liang3, Liang Liu1

1The Hong Kong Polytechnic University   2The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)   3University of Electronic Science and Technology of China

RadioWorld adapts a pretrained world model to synthesize radio-frequency radiance fields directly from 3D scene geometry and transceiver locations, without any reference RF measurements at inference.

RadioWorld synthesizes received signal strength maps at different heights and spatial spectrum diagrams at arbitrary locations from scene geometry and transceiver locations.

Method

A dual-stream Mixture-of-Transformers turns a pretrained world model into a feed-forward RF field synthesizer. One stream understands the wireless environment. The other generates the radio field.

Scene-Understanding Transformer

Encodes a bird’s-eye view of building occupancy, height, and transceiver locations into environment-aware electromagnetic propagation features. The same stream can also describe a link in text, including line-of-sight and the dominant arrival and departure directions.

Field-Generation Transformer

Attends to those features at every layer and synthesizes the requested field from local conditions: a planar slice for a multi-height RSSM, or a spherical panorama for bidirectional SSDs. Because RSSM and SSD in a fixed scene are deterministic, multi-step flow matching is replaced by a single-step readout.

The understanding stream keeps causal self-attention. The generation stream uses full attention over scene features, geometric conditions, coordinates, and learnable queries.

Results

On UrbanRF-Bench, 657 scenes and 32,850 radiance fields across seven cities, RadioWorld is evaluated zero-shot in Beijing and Washington. RSSM baselines are retrained on the five training cities. SSD baselines receive 500 reference measurements per scene. RadioWorld uses zero reference measurements.
